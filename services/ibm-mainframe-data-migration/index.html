IBM Mainframe Data Migration

Modernize Legacy Systems, Migrate With Confidence

Legacy Modernization Partner

Move off the mainframe without losing what makes it reliable

Sri Bharathi Tech helps organizations migrate IBM mainframe applications and data to modern platforms. We work with COBOL and PL/1 codebases, CICS transaction systems, and JCL batch workflows to plan and execute migrations that preserve business logic while reducing dependence on legacy infrastructure.

On the data side, we migrate DB2, IMS, and VSAM data stores to modern relational and cloud-native databases, with a focus on data integrity, validation, and reconciliation at every stage of the migration.

Mainframe-to-Cloud Transformation

Beyond one-time migrations, we help teams replatform mainframe workloads to cloud infrastructure, reducing operational overhead while keeping critical systems available throughout the transition.
