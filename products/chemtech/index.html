Cutting-Edge Molecule Generation for Drug Discovery with Robust Data Privacy

Welcome to the world of ChemTech, an innovative solution developed by Sri Bharathi Tech, designed to revolutionize the drug discovery process. ChemTech harnesses the power of generative AI to create a state-of-the-art molecule generation model. At Sri Bharathi Tech, we prioritize data privacy, ensuring secure handling of proprietary information through robust encryption and compliance with industry standards.

Transform your research and development activities!

The algorithm employs CMA-ES for efficient navigation of chemical space. Through iterative steps of molecule generation, property evaluation, and model refinement, it seamlessly integrates with the ChemTrack Cloud API for scalable deployment. Researchers can customize objective functions to suit unique research needs, providing precise control over molecule generation. This ensures effective exploration and optimization across varied chemical landscapes.

ChemTech can transform your research and development activities, saving valuable time and resources while maintaining the highest standards of data security.
